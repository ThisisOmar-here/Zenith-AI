# Sleep routines and small daily practices

## Sleep
- Keep the same wake-up time every day, including weekends.
- Use the bed for sleep only. If you are awake and frustrated for a long time, get up, do something calm in dim light, and return when sleepy.
- Write a short "brain dump" list before bed so worries are parked on paper.
- Reduce screens and caffeine in the hours before sleep.

## Gratitude and pleasant activities
Writing down three specific good things at the end of the day trains attention toward what is going well. Planning one small enjoyable activity and one small achievement each day (sometimes called pleasure and mastery) helps when motivation and mood are low.
