# Handling setbacks

Resilience is less about never struggling and more about how we recover.

- Separate the event from your identity: "I failed this exam" is not "I am a failure."
- Ask what is in your control right now, and act on one small part of it.
- Look for meaning or learning without rushing it. Some lessons only become clear later.
- Treat yourself the way you would treat a friend in the same situation. Self-compassion is linked to getting back up faster, not to giving up.
- Stay connected. Talking to people you trust is one of the strongest buffers against stress.
