# Active listening and "I" statements

Many arguments are less about the topic and more about not feeling heard.

## Reflect before you respond
Repeat back the core of what the other person said in your own words: "So you felt ignored when I was on my phone at dinner?" Then let them correct you. People calm down noticeably once they feel understood.

## Use "I" statements
Describe your experience instead of judging theirs: "I feel worried when plans change at the last minute" instead of "You never think about anyone else." Name the feeling, the situation, and what would help.

## Pick the moment
Hard conversations go better when both people are rested and not in a rush. It is fine to say "I want to talk about this properly. Can we do it tonight?"
